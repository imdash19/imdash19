> which doesn't reflect a developer's true commitment and consistency.
>
> **My approach:** I focus on **total contributions**, **contribution density**, and
> **weekly consistency** rather than a raw daily streak number. A single rest day

>
> 📊 **What actually matters:**
> - Total annual contributions (see stats card above)
> - Contribution frequency & density (see activity graph below)
> - Long-term trend (weeks & months, not just consecutive days)
